AI in Cybersecurity

      
AI is reshaping both attack and defense. Here are the new roles, tools, and concepts every security professional needs to know in 2025+.

    

    

      
 Why AI Matters in Cyber

      
AI is now used on both sides — defenders use it for anomaly detection, malware triage, and automated response. Attackers use it for phishing generation, deepfakes, automated exploit discovery, and prompt injection against LLM apps. Understanding AI security is no longer optional — it's a core competency.

Hacking Arsenal is an educational platform. Every tool, payload and technique here must only be used against systems you own or have explicit written permission to test.
